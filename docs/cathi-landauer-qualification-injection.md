## Attribution Guardrail

This is the most important rule for this draft.

The existing website often moves between:

- the founder
- ISS leadership
- the ISS team
- company past performance

That means some statements are safe to attribute directly to Cathi, while others are safer as founder-led or team-led support claims.

Use this rule:

- If the website explicitly ties something to the founder, Cathi can usually own that claim directly.
- If the website presents something as ISS or team delivery, it can support Cathi's profile as leadership proof, but should not automatically be rewritten as `Cathi personally did X` unless that attribution is already clear.

This matters especially for:

- team-wide certifications
- broad company capability claims across all six service lines
- platform and tool expertise listed at the firm level
- newer website implementation claims written as ISS capability rather than Cathi-specific background
