ISS approaches cybersecurity and compliance from an operational foundation — not paper-based compliance consulting. The team's security experience begins with hands-on classified-environment work: configuring encryption devices for live missile defense events, operating in NSA-affiliated commands, and designing secure communications architectures where failure had real consequences.

## Named delivery

At SPAWAR, ISS configured KG-84 encryption devices, established SHF satellite communications, and designed alternate EHF/SHF communications paths that enabled launch-on-TADIL scenarios for BMD and THAAD test events. At the U.S. Navy's NIOC San Diego — an NSA-affiliated command — ISS operated classified SIGINT collection and exploitation systems. This operational background informs how ISS approaches NIST RMF, FISMA, and FedRAMP alignment: not as abstract compliance documentation, but as security controls grounded in how classified systems actually operate.

## How ISS works differently

Most cybersecurity vendors working with small and mid-size federal programs offer compliance documentation services. ISS offers that — but with the credibility of a team that has personally configured the encryption hardware, operated in the classified environments, and designed the communications architectures that security frameworks exist to protect. The difference shows in implementation quality: controls that reflect operational reality, not templated language.

## Where this capability fits

This capability fits programs that need security and compliance support backed by real classified-environment past performance — especially ATO efforts, FISMA-reportable systems, or modernization programs where compliance cannot be a late-stage afterthought.
