ISS treats digital experience and custom solution work as part of program delivery — not a standalone web or app project. That means user experience, workflow performance, accessibility compliance, and integration with enterprise systems all stay in view from the start.

## Named delivery

At DOI/BLM, ISS built a national enterprise resource management prototype on the ArcGIS platform — a GIS-based web application designed to serve one of the largest land-managing agencies in the federal government (245 million acres). This was not a mockup; it was a functional prototype integrating enterprise geospatial data that had been converted from legacy CAD, PDF, and JPEG formats into enterprise-ready structures. The ISS website itself demonstrates this capability: a modern, accessible, performance-optimized web application built on Astro and deployed on Cloudflare Workers, designed to meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.

## How ISS works differently

Many digital vendors build applications in isolation and hand them off. ISS builds digital solutions inside broader modernization programs — which means the application connects to enterprise data, the accessibility requirements are met from day one (not patched after audit), and the solution fits the organization's larger technology roadmap. The team's ArcGIS Enterprise implementation experience at DOI/BLM means GIS web applications are built by a team that understands the underlying enterprise geospatial infrastructure, not just the front-end display layer.

## Where this capability fits

This capability is the right match when a program needs custom digital tools — especially GIS-based web or mobile applications — built by a team that understands federal accessibility requirements, enterprise data integration, and how digital projects fit within larger modernization efforts.
