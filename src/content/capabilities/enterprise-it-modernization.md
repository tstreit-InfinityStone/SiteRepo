ISS has delivered enterprise modernization across federal and regulated environments — not as a platform reseller, but as the implementation partner responsible for bridging requirements, policy, and technical execution in programs where failure creates real operational consequences.

## Named delivery

At DOI/BLM, ISS led HR benefits system adoption, enterprise ticketing deployment, UNIX-to-Linux server migration, EIS network migration, ePlanning modernization requirements definition, and cloud migration preparation — across a nationally distributed agency spanning 12 states with field offices that could not absorb downtime. At SourceGas, ISS migrated the ticketing system from SharePoint to Exchange, created SCCM imaging packages for rapid workstation provisioning across remote sites, and resolved a legacy support backlog within 60 days.

## How ISS works differently

Most modernization vendors specialize in either strategy or execution. ISS operates in the middle ground where modernization decisions, platform realities, stakeholder alignment, and support implications all have to be reconciled simultaneously. The team's program management credentials (PMP, SAFe, ScrumMaster) mean modernization planning stays grounded in delivery discipline — not just roadmap slides.

## Where this capability fits

This capability is the right match when an enterprise needs to retire unsupported platforms, adopt new systems without losing operational continuity, or recover from a stalled modernization effort with a delivery team that has done it before — at named agencies, with verifiable outcomes.
