ISS's program management approach is rooted in military operational discipline — not just methodology certification. The team holds PMP, SAFe, ScrumMaster, ITIL, and 25+ total professional credentials. But what distinguishes ISS is where that discipline was forged: writing test plans for live missile defense events where test failure had real operational and safety consequences.

## Named delivery

At SPAWAR, ISS authored comprehensive test plans for live BMD (Ballistic Missile Defense) and THAAD test events, serving as systems operator and network integrator. This included post-event message accuracy analysis and SOP-based training for integration scenarios. The work was recognized with multiple government agency awards.

At DOI/BLM, ISS delivered ePlanning modernization requirements definition and led strategic planning for a national enterprise resource management prototype — coordinating requirements across technical, policy, and executive stakeholders in a nationally distributed agency.

## How ISS works differently

Most program management vendors lead with certifications. ISS has the certifications — but the delivery discipline comes from operational environments where "failed delivery" meant a missile defense test event didn't execute, a SIGINT collection mission didn't produce, or a federal agency's distributed operations went offline. That standard of accountability carries into every civilian and commercial engagement. The team doesn't treat requirements ambiguity as normal — they treat it as a risk to be resolved before it becomes a delivery failure.

## Where this capability fits

This capability fits programs that need structured delivery support from a team whose past performance includes the highest-accountability environments in federal service. If your program has competing stakeholder inputs, unclear requirements, or recovery from a prior delivery failure, ISS brings the coordination discipline to get it moving again.
